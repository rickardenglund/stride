How to export from Garmin Connect
  1. Set your Garmin Connect units to metric (kilometers).
  2. Open Activities → All Activities in Garmin Connect on the web.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the list, choose Export CSV, and import the file here.

CSV files without unit labels are treated as kilometers.

You can add GPX routes, heart rate, and elevation after importing your activities.
